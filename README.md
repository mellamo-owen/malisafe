### **🎯 Shida Tunatatua**
Duka 40,000+ Kenya zinapoteza **KES 2B+ kila mwaka** kwa wizi na uharibifu. Insurance ya kawaida ni:
- **Ghali**: KES 2,000+/month premiums
- **Slow**: Claims zinachukua weeks
- **Complex**: Karatasi nyingi, wakala, inspection

Matokeo: 95% ya duka ndogo hawana bima.

### **💡 Solution Yetu: MaliSafe**
**MaliSafe App** - Weka bidhaa, lipa bima ndogo, dai kwa dakika kupitia M-Pesa.
- **Gharama**: Kuanzia **KES 100** per claim - hakuna monthly fee
- **Speed**: Claims zina-settle in **<24 hours**  
- **Trust**: Backed by **Direct Assurance Insurance** - Policy `DA-202606-`
- **Simple**: Picha 2 + M-Pesa = claim submitted

## **⚙️ Jinsi MaliSafe Inafanya Kazi**

**Hatua 4 rahisi - Duka hadi M-Pesa in < 2 minutes:**

### **1. Sajili Duka & Bidhaa 📱**
Fungua MaliSafe App → Weka jina la duka + picha ya bidhaa. 
- **Mfano**: Samsung TV KES 49,997
- **System inatengeneza**: MaliSafe ID `ML-2026-LYKE` automatically

### **2. Bidhaa Inapata Bima Instant 🛡️**
MaliSafe inaunganisha na **Direct Assurance** nyuma ya pazia.
- **Policy Number**: `DA-202606-223237` ina-generate
- **Status**: `Active` mara moja
- **Gharama**: Hakuna malipo ya mwezi - unalipa ukidai tu

### **3. Wizi/Uharibifu Ukitokea 😰**
Fungua app → **"Wasilisha Madai"** → Lipa **KES 100** kupitia M-Pesa
- **Upload**: Picha ya polisi report ama bidhaa iliyoharibika
- **Click**: Submit - inaenda Direct Assurance direct

### **4. Pesa Inaingiia M-Pesa Yako 💸**
Direct Assurance inaverify in **<24 hours**
- **Approved**: Pesa ya TV KES 49,997 inatumwa kwa M-Pesa yako
- **SMS**: "Claim DA-202606-223237 imelipwa. Asante kwa kutumia MaliSafe"

**Mfano Halisi:**
> Amina ana duka Moi's Bridge. TV yake iliibiwa usiku.  
> 9:00am: Alifungua MaliSafe, akalipa KES 100, akasubmit claim.  
> 3:00pm: KES 49,997 iliingia M-Pesa yake.  
> **Total time: 6 hours. Total cost: KES 100.**
